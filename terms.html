Prayer times and Qibla

  • Times are calculated astronomically on your device: in Saudi Arabia by the Umm al-Qura calendar, elsewhere by the method you choose or the app suggests for your country.
  • Times may differ by a minute or two from other sources depending on location, method and madhab.
  • The Qibla direction depends on your location and your device’s compass accuracy, and can be affected by nearby metal or magnets.
  • Hijri dates follow Umm al-Qura; Ramadan, Eid and Arafah dates are subject to official announcements.
  • Alerts are an aid and may be delayed by the system or battery saving — do not rely on them alone for worship.

  • النص القرآني: الإصدار الرقمي للرسم العثماني برواية حفص من مجمع الملك فهد لطباعة المصحف الشريف، دون تعديل.
  • التفسير: «تيسير الكريم الرحمن» للشيخ عبدالرحمن بن ناصر السعدي رحمه الله، و«التفسير الميسر» من إصدار مجمع الملك فهد لطباعة المصحف الشريف.
  • الأذكار والأدعية: من الكتاب والسنة الصحيحة مع ذكر مصادرها.

Religious content and sources

  • Mushaf pages: the Madinah Mushaf (Hafs), 1441 AH print, from the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex, under the free-use terms the Complex provides.
  • Quran text: the digital Uthmanic Hafs text by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex, unmodified.
  • Tafsir: “Taysir al-Karim al-Rahman” by Sheikh Abdulrahman bin Nasser As-Saadi, and “Al-Tafsir Al-Muyassar” by a group of scholars under the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex.
  • Adhkar and supplications: from the Quran and authentic Sunnah, with sources cited.
  • Recitations from EveryAyah; adhan and audio recordings belong to their owners.

We take care with accuracy — if you notice an error, contact us and we will correct it promptly.

  1. Payment is charged to your Google Play or App Store account when you confirm the purchase.
